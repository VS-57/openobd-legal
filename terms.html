What the app does

OpenOBD connects to a Wi-Fi or Bluetooth Low Energy ELM327-compatible OBD2 adapter that you plug into your vehicle, and shows live data, fault codes and related information. You need your own compatible adapter; adapters made to work only with their own app are generally not compatible. The app also has a demo mode that does not need an adapter. The app reads data from your vehicle; it does not write settings or software to vehicle control units. The one exception you can use is clearing stored fault codes, which is only done when you ask for it.

Clearing fault codes

Clearing fault codes also clears related stored data and resets readiness monitors. This can hide a real problem and may affect emissions inspections. Clear codes only if you understand what they mean. You do this at your own risk.
