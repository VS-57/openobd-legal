OpenOBD — Support

OpenOBD turns your iPhone into a real-time car dashboard using an OBD2 (ELM327) adapter.

Getting started

  1. Plug an ELM327 OBD2 adapter into your car’s OBD2 port.
  2. Open OpenOBD and tap Connect.
  3. Choose your adapter (Bluetooth or Wi-Fi). To try the app without hardware, choose Demo Mode.

Features

  • Live engine data (speed, RPM, coolant, voltage, fuel and more)
  • Trip logging with GPS route, including in the background
  • Diagnostics: read and clear fault codes (DTCs)
  • English and Turkish

Troubleshooting

  • Can’t find the adapter? Make sure it’s plugged in, the ignition is on, and Bluetooth is enabled. For Wi-Fi adapters, join the adapter’s Wi-Fi network first.
  • No data? Some values depend on your specific vehicle and may not be supported by all cars.
